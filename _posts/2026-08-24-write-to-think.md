title: "Write to think, not to explain"
categories: [ideas]
---

Most of my notes never get read by anyone else, and that's fine — the point wasn't to explain something, it was to figure out what I actually think. Writing forces the vague idea in my head to become a sentence, and sentences are easy to spot holes in.

If an idea survives being written down badly, it's usually worth writing down well.
